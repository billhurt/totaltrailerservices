Mobile trailer servicing across West Norfolk and the Fens

I'm based near King's Lynn and travel out to the trailer, whether that's on the coast at Hunstanton or over the Cambridgeshire line at Littleport. Pick your town for what's worth knowing about getting a service booked there.

Trailer servicing in King's Lynn

Home base · where the A149, A47 and A10 meet

This is home ground. Being based just outside town means King's Lynn gets the most flexibility, and a weekday evening slot is usually the easiest to fit in here because there's no long drive either side of the job.

Plenty of trailers round here spend most of their time parked behind a house, in a yard or at the edge of a field between jobs. That's exactly where the service happens: as long as there's room to get round the wheels, there's no need to move it.

Includes Gaywood, South Wootton, Terrington St Clement, Watlington and the villages around the town.

Hunstanton

About 16 miles north on the A149

Trailers on the coast have a harder life. Old Hunstanton, Heacham and Snettisham are all covered.

Launch from the beach? Boat and jet-ski trailers that get backed into salt water are the classic case. Water gets into the hubs, bearings corrode, and brake shoes and cables seize if they're left wet. A service that pays proper attention to bearings and brakes is worth doing before the season starts.

Mobile trailer servicing in Wisbech

About 13 miles west across the Fens on the A47

Wisbech and the villages round it (Walsoken, Emneth, Outwell, Upwell) have plenty of trailers that earn their keep: flatbeds and box trailers moving machinery, landscaping materials, market stock and kit between sites.

Working trailers tend to get used until something stops working. A regular service picks up worn bearings and tired brakes before they leave you stuck at the side of a Fen road with a load on. If you run more than one trailer, mention it when you get in touch so they can be looked at on the same visit.

Downham Market

About 11 miles south on the A10

Straight down the A10, so Downham, Denver, Hilgay and Stow Bardolph are an easy run for an evening booking.

If you tow a horse trailer, it's almost certainly a twin axle braked trailer, and with live weight on board you want brakes that pull evenly and bearings that aren't running hot. Both are part of the twin axle general service at £100, done in your yard without the trailer leaving it.

Littleport

About 24 miles down the A10, in Cambridgeshire

The southern end of the regular area, just over the county line. Because of the distance, bookings here usually work best at weekends. Southery and Brandon Creek on the way down are covered too.

A trailer that hasn't moved since last summer needs its brakes and bearings checked before its first tow, not after.

Trailer servicing in Swaffham

About 15 miles east on the A47

Swaffham sits where the A47 crosses the A1065, so trailers here often do longer runs than a trip to the tip and back. Distance is hard on bearings. The grease breaks down and wear builds faster on a trailer that regularly does thirty or forty miles at a time.

Also covers Sporle, Narborough, Castle Acre and nearby villages.

Dereham

About 27 miles east along the A47

The furthest east the service regularly goes. Dereham bookings are mostly at weekends, and it's worth grouping jobs: if a neighbour, a club or a business down the road has trailers due too, say so when you message and they can be done on the same trip.

Includes Toftwood, Scarning and Swanton Morley.
