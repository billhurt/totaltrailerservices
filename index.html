No workshop drop-off. No price that changes on the day.

Getting a trailer serviced usually means hitching it up, towing it to a workshop and leaving it there, often without knowing what it'll cost. Total Trailer Services works the other way round.

I come to wherever the trailer is kept, carry out the service there, and charge the fixed price for your type of trailer. If I find a fault that needs more than a service, I'll explain what it is and give you a price before any extra work.

Find your price by counting the wheels

Every general service has a fixed price, set by how many axles your trailer has and whether it has brakes. Horseboxes and food trailers are priced the same way: count the wheels.

Braked trailers have a spring-loaded overrun coupling and a thin breakaway cable at the front. If yours has neither, it's unbraked.
