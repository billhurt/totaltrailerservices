From RAF ground equipment to your trailer

Total Trailer Services is my own business, set up to give trailer owners around King's Lynn a properly qualified, fully mobile service at a fixed, fair price.

My background

I've worked in engineering for almost 20 years, over ten of them as a Ground Equipment Engineer in the Royal Air Force, which is still my full-time job.

That work teaches you to do a job fully and in order: inspect, find the actual cause, fix it, check it again. I bring the same approach to every trailer.

Why trailers, and why mobile

Trailers get forgotten until something goes wrong. Most owners don't want to tow a trailer to a workshop and leave it there just to find out whether the bearings are fine. So I come to you, do the work where the trailer is parked, and charge a price you can see before you book.

For now that means general servicing and fault diagnosis: brakes, bearings, lights and wiring. I'd rather do a few things properly than offer everything from day one. The range of services will grow over time, and new services will show up on the services page with prices once they're available.
